Forest to Fortune

A first-person lumber idle game for PC and Android, by Amelia Hazelwood.

Chop trees, sell the logs, and grow a one-axe clearing into a lumber empire: sawmills, carpentry workshops and cabin builders that keep working while you're away. Sign in to keep your saves on all your devices.
