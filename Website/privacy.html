Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short: Forest to Fortune keeps your progress on your device. If you choose to sign in for cloud saves, your saves and the account details needed to sign you in are stored with Unity, the company whose services we use. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and we don't sell or share your information for advertising.

Who we are

Forest to Fortune ("the game") is made by Hazelwood ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your information, email lunalazum18@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (save slots A, B and C) and your settings as files on your device. They never leave your device unless you turn on cloud saves. You can erase a save at any time from the save screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the game.

Cloud saves (optional)

Cloud saves are off until you choose to sign in. When you sign in with a Unity Player Account (using Google, Apple or an email address), the following is processed by Unity Technologies to provide the feature:

  • Account details: the email address of your Unity Player Account and a player ID that Unity creates for you. The game shows your email in Settings so you can see which account you're signed in with.
  • Your game progress: the contents of your save slots (money, upgrades, machines, rebirths, achievements, statistics such as trees felled and time played), so you can continue on another device.
  • Technical data: Unity's services may receive basic technical information needed to run them, such as your device type, operating system, IP address and the version of their software, and diagnostics if something goes wrong.

Unity handles this information under its own Privacy Policy. We use your saves only to keep them in sync between your devices. You can sign out at any time in Settings > Account; your saves then stay on your device only.

Discord status (Windows, optional)

On Windows, if the Discord app is running on your computer, the game can show what you're doing in it on your Discord profile (for example "Chopping with the Lumberjack's Axe", your in-game money and rebirths). This is sent only to the Discord app on your computer, which shares it under Discord's Privacy Policy. Turn it off any time in Settings > Interface > Discord status.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No in-app purchases or payment information.
  • We don't sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Android permissions

On Android the game uses internet access (only for cloud saves and signing in) and vibration (for touch feedback, which you can turn off in Settings > Controls).

How long information is kept

Files on your device stay until you delete them or uninstall the game. Cloud saves stay until you delete the slot in the game (which also deletes it from the cloud while you're signed in) or ask us to delete your cloud data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a save: use Delete save on the save screen.
  • Delete all your cloud data: email us from the address of your Unity Player Account and we'll delete it, normally within 30 days.
  • Delete your Unity Player Account: do this from Unity's player account portal, or contact Unity as described in their Privacy Policy.
  • Access or correct your information: email us. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as to receive a copy of your data or to object to how it's used, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The game can be played without signing in. If you believe a child has given us personal information through cloud saves, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Cloud saves are sent to Unity over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we only keep what the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
